STUDIO

A Practice Built Around Spatial Clarity

Time & Joy Space Design approaches interiors through order, proportion, light, and restrained material decisions. The work is less about statement and more about building environments that stay calm, readable, and durable over time.

Commercial projects lead the practice, while residential work supports the studio's point of view on atmosphere, discipline, and lived proportion.

DESIGN POSITION

We prefer spaces that unfold with clarity. Circulation should feel natural, light should act as structure, and materials should support calm rather than compete for attention.

SERVICE FOCUS

Current work focuses on hospitality, workplace, retail, and private residential interiors where spatial order, long-term use, and execution quality matter.

PROJECT RANGE

The studio is based in Chengdu and works across projects in other cities when the brief, schedule, and collaboration rhythm are aligned.

COLLABORATION APPROACH

How We Work

01

We begin by clarifying the brief, the site's operational logic, and the emotional quality the project should hold over time.

02

Space planning, proportion, and circulation are resolved before decorative decisions, so the project reads clearly at its foundation.

03

Material and lighting decisions stay controlled, allowing each layer of the project to reinforce the same spatial attitude.

04

Execution matters as much as concept. We stay attentive to detailing, coordination, and the final reading of the completed environment.

SUITABILITY

The practice is best suited to clients who value clarity, restraint, and a slower but more durable design logic.
