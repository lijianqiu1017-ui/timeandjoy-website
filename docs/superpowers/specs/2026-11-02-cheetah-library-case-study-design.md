# Cheetah Library Case Study Design

## Scope

Publish the Cheetah Library case study alongside the already prepared Digital Specialized Studio case study. Both projects will be added to the home page, project directory, project detail data, and sitemap.

## Project Facts

- Name: Cheetah Library / 猎豹书苑
- Location: Chengdu, Sichuan, China
- Year: 2024
- Area: 800 square metres
- Type: Commercial / Cultural Reading Space
- Scope: Interior Design

## Narrative Direction

The case study follows the confirmed direction, "Living With Timber / Poetic Dwelling." It begins at the library's threshold, then moves through the central reading hall, the flowing ceiling and timber shelves, and closes on the softer relationship between stepped reading, planting, and extended stays.

The writing will remain restrained and spatial. It will describe light, timber, landscape, and reading behaviour without relying on functional or promotional language.

## Image Treatment

All seven supplied images will be published from a project-specific English, lowercase, kebab-case asset directory.

- No image may be cropped.
- The hero image preserves its original aspect ratio.
- Every gallery image is displayed in a single full-width row.
- Image containers must not hide any part of an image.
- Gallery images must render with `object-fit: contain`, natural height, no maximum-height constraint, and no hover scaling.
